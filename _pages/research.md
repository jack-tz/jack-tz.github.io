## Current Research

### Automated Security Analysis of Cloud Environments

*PhD Research | 2026 – Present*

My current research investigates automated techniques
for identifying privilege escalation paths in cloud
environments. In particular, I am interested in reasoning
about security risks arising from the interactions between
identity and access management, network connectivity,
compute workloads and cloud services.

## Previous Research

### LLM-Assisted Stateful Fuzzing

*Master's Research | 2025*

This research explored the application of large language
models to stateful fuzzing of network protocols, with
a particular focus on identifying state-controlling
variables and improving protocol state inference.
